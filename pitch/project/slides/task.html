The task: close a term sheet by e-mail, scored on total cost

The score

(C open − C deal) ÷ (C open − C best), clipped to 0..1. A deal worse than the opening quote or a broken must-have scores zero. No deal scores zero.

What C counts

  • Price over the horizon, and the old price until the effective date
  • Payment days, freight, late delivery, tooling
  • Index-clause risk, quality commitments
  • Supplier health, in strategic categories

The opponent

  • LLM supplier bots with hidden floors
  • Ten rounds per thread, 60 s per message
  • A final offer that walks if pushed
  • RFQs with three suppliers at once

Live final: five waves of eight minutes, one attempt per case, all teams at once

Only term sheets bind. The unit price is one of five cost parts, so a lower price with a worse clause can lose. That framing decides everything the agent does.
