Four steps, all deterministic rules

1 · Prepare

Cited evidence

From the data package: cost-driver indices, our purchase history, should-cost, scorecards, competing bids, contract notice periods. Each claim carries the IDs the arena verifies.

2 · Read

Standing sheets and status

Which of our asks the supplier refused or repriced, whether it is short of cash, whether it sent a final offer. Never the leaderboard or results.

3 · Decide

Price every package

Anchor at 0.80 of the ask, match the supplier's visible concession, send one to three equal-cost sheets that trade levers. Must-haves enforced by a guard on every sheet.

4 · Close

Complete package or nothing

Accept when the whole package beats the opening and the best alternative in the pessimistic world. Take a passing final offer at once. Never walk while a supplier is alive.

In strategic categories the price is set on a supplier-specific health estimate with an uncertainty band

No language model in the loop. Everything the agent says is a template plus verified facts; everything it decides is the cost function plus a few rules we can point to in the code.
