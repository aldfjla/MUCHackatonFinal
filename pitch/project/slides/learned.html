What we learned about the suppliers, from 400 logged sessions

They are deterministic

100 sessions, ten per case, reproduced every deal to the cent. One session per experiment is a perfect measurement.

The final offer has a fixed clock

Each supplier sends it at the same message number, third to sixth, and its size scales with our last step. Pushing past it makes them walk.

Evidence moves them only when it is new

"We did not know that, a fair point" on notice periods and energy costs. Labour indices are acknowledged and ignored.

The clause buys the price

A copper-exposed motor maker took 364.65 with an index clause at once and held 374 without one. Offer the clause they want, not the one we like.

Supplier health is a cliff

3.6 % under a supplier-specific price the health charge rose sixfold. Our band protects against it; scaling the term down walked us off the edge.

Hard floors exist

Raw-material and RFQ suppliers sit on floors no anchor, timing or payment term moved. The best cost in those cases is below any offer they ever made.

Sources: our own transcripts (agent/arena_kit/sim_cases/real_runs_*), replays, and the arena's cost breakdowns per deal

All six come from our transcripts and the arena's own cost breakdown of each deal, not from guesses. The determinism finding changed how we test: breadth, not repeats.
