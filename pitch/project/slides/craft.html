Method

Candidate vs unchanged baseline on identical cases; promote only what repeats; rejected ideas stay as default-off flags with their result in the comment. A fitted mock arena for crash and regression checks, never for tuning: it is in-sample and text-blind.

Explainability

Every decision writes its rule, the cost breakdown of the package, the health band and the concession history into the result. Every e-mail cites only facts the arena can verify; zero contradicted claims across all sessions.

If asked about the mock: it was fitted on our own transcripts and could not predict any real-arena result, so we stopped trusting it for decisions and kept it for regression checks.
