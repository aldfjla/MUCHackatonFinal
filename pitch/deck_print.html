01 · the job

Close a term sheet by e-mail. Get scored on the whole cost.

score

(C_open − C_deal) / (C_open − C_best)

clipped to 0..1. Worse than the opening quote, a broken must-have, or no deal: 0.

what C counts

  • price over the horizon, old price until the effective date
  • payment days · freight · late delivery · tooling
  • index-clause risk · quality commitments
  • supplier health, in strategic categories

the opponent

  • LLM supplier bots with hidden floors
  • ten rounds per thread, 60 s per message
  • a final offer that walks if pushed
  • RFQs with three suppliers at once

live final · 5 waves × 8 min · one attempt per case · all teams at once

02 · the pipeline

Four stages per message, all deterministic rules

● prepare

Cited evidence

Cost-driver indices, our purchase history, should-cost, scorecards, competing bids, contract notice periods. Each claim carries the IDs the arena verifies.

→

● read

Sheets and status

Which asks the supplier refused or repriced, whether it is short of cash, whether it sent a final offer. Never the leaderboard or results.

→

● decide

Price every package

Anchor at 0.80 of the ask, match the supplier's visible concession, send one to three equal-cost sheets that trade levers. A guard enforces must-haves on every sheet.

→

● close

Whole package or nothing

Accept when the package beats the opening and the best alternative in the pessimistic world. Take a passing final offer at once. Never walk while a supplier is alive.

07 · supplier model · from 403 logged sessions

What we learned about the suppliers, and from where

deterministic

100 of 100 sessions identical

Ten passes over ten cases reproduced every deal to the cent. One session per experiment is a perfect measurement; repeats are wasted quota.

fixed clock

Final offer at message 3 to 6

Each supplier sends it at the same message number every time; its size scales with our last step. Push past it and they walk.

evidence

Moves them only when it is new

"We did not know that, a fair point" on notice periods and energy costs. Labour indices: acknowledged in every case, acted on in none.

clause

The clause buys the price

A copper-exposed motor maker took 364.65 with an index clause at once and held 374 without one, three runs out of three.

cliff

Supplier health is a cliff

3.6 % under a supplier-specific price the health charge rose sixfold. Our band protects against it; scaling the term down walked us off the edge.

floors

Some floors do not move

Raw-material and RFQ suppliers held against every anchor, timing and payment term we tried. There the best cost sits below any offer they ever made.

sources: our transcripts (agent/arena_kit/sim_cases/real_runs_*) · replays · the arena's cost breakdown per deal · LEARNINGS_v36.md

09 · transfer

Would it work on a real supplier?

● carries over

  • Buying on total cost over the horizon, not unit price
  • Every claim cited from our own data, so a buyer can stand behind the e-mail
  • Package trades: payment, contract length, effective date, index clause
  • An explicit supplier-health limit in strategic categories
  • A log of why each offer was made and why each deal was accepted

● does not, yet

  • Real suppliers are not deterministic and do not announce a final offer
  • The agent reads structure, not text: an objection only registers when it changes a sheet
  • Fixed-clock timing rules would be wrong against a human
  • No memory across negotiations with the same supplier

next stage

A language model that turns an objection into a structured hypothesis (what they value, what they refuse, what is uncertain), with the arithmetic and the accept checks staying deterministic. We built the deterministic half and tested it; the interpretation half is the open work.

10 · craft

One change at a time, judged by the real arena

method

Candidate vs unchanged baseline on identical cases; promote only what repeats; rejected ideas stay as default-off flags with their result in the comment. A fitted mock arena for crash and regression checks, never for tuning: it is in-sample and text-blind.

explainability

Every decision writes its rule, the package cost breakdown, the health band and the concession history into the result. Every e-mail cites only facts the arena can verify; zero contradicted claims across all sessions.
