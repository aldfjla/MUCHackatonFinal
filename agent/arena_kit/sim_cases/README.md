# sim_cases

- `S01-S03.json`: the three cases of the plain arena-sim script.
- `real/`: the 21 real case files fetched from the arena (P01-P19, D01, D02), read only.
- `real_runs/`: dumps of real practice sessions (agent, result, all threads); the evidence of `supplier_profile.json`.
- `supplier_profile.json`: the fitted real-like supplier (`python -m arena_kit.fit_supplier`); each parameter is
  `fitted` (n samples, evidence, uncertainty) or `assumed`.
- `gen_dev/` (30 cases) and `gen_test/` (30 cases): generated by `python -m arena_kit.gen_cases --seed 1` in the
  schema of `real/`, from other supplier/part pairs of the data package. Tune on `gen_dev`; score on `gen_test`
  once, never tune on it.

**What "held out" means here.** `gen_test` is held out only in case content: its parts are disjoint from
`gen_dev` and each (parts, suppliers) combination is drawn at most twice per set. It is NOT held out in
supplier behaviour: both sets run the same `supplier_profile.json`, fitted on the same real runs, so an agent
that exploits a quirk of the profile wins on both. Five suppliers appear in both sets (sup:70120, 70180, 70340,
70440, 70740), because few suppliers sell several free parts. The pool is small: 55 free supplier/part pairs
with a price and orders, 28 of them used by real cases; dev uses 23 pairs and test 17.

How a generated case is made (see `gen_cases.py`): a real case of the same type is the template (negotiable
fields, options, must-have kind, alternative kind); a free pair replaces its parties; the current price is the
pair's latest `price_conditions` row, the need comes from its purchase orders (x 0.7-1.4), money rates scale with
price and spend, refs are real IDs (contract, scorecard, quote, doc, cost). A type with no free combination left
(bundling: one supplier per set sells 3 free parts) gives its slots to other types, so the type mix is not even.

Varied beyond what the 21 real cases show (assumed, not observed): interest rate 0.04-0.12 in 30 % of the
cases (real: 0.08 only), horizon 3 years in about 10 % (real: 1 and 2), category goal swapped from the
template's in 25 % of cases, must-have drawn from the negotiable fields, alternative kinds limited to those the
real cases of the type have. A real-quote alternative needs a real quote of the same part from another supplier:
the test set has few.

**What the supplier profile rests on.** Statuses: `fitted` (3 or more cases or threads), `fitted_n1` (one or two:
an anecdote), `assumed` (no evidence, or only bounds). The floors partly derive from the arena's own C_best
(each real case's C_best is inverted to the supplier's cash floor with `cost.supplier_value`), so they inherit its
assumptions. P05 (three suppliers) is excluded from the floor fit: our C_open recomputation misses the arena's by
-11 %. The standard payment days are read per supplier from its e-mails (30 for most, 45 for P05 sup:70240, 60 fixed
in P11); a thread that never states them defaults to 30 (4 of 12 threads, assumed).

Evaluate: `python -m arena_kit.evaluate --agents a,b --set dev --seeds 5`. `--set real` also compares with the real
scores of the same agents; that comparison is IN-SAMPLE (the profile was fitted on those runs). Mock results are a
robustness check, not a score forecast.
